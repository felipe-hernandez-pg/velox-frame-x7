Progress Tracking

Progressive Overload Rules:
• Once you hit the top of the rep range for all sets → add 2.5kg (upper) or 5kg (lower)
• Track your weights weekly — even small jumps compound over 90 days
• If you miss the bottom of the rep range on 2+ sets → keep the weight, focus form
• Data saves automatically to your device (localStorage)
